Pocket Sudoku

The classic number puzzle — clean, offline, and free. Available on Google Play as Sudoku: Classic Puzzle.

Four difficulty levels, a new Daily Challenge every day, 31 achievements, pencil notes, statistics, and QR code puzzle sharing. Plays fully offline, in 7 languages.

Privacy policy

Pocket Sudoku does not require an account and does not ask you for personal information. There is no sign-in, no profile, and no server of ours that your data is sent to.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved game, statistics, achievements, and daily streak
  • Your settings — language, sound, reminder time, and preferences

All of it is stored locally in the app's private storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We never receive it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve them, Google may collect information such as your advertising ID, IP address, and general device and app information. This is handled by Google under its own policies, not by us — see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can remove ads entirely with a one-time in-app purchase.

Purchases

The optional "remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play — the app never sees or stores your card or billing information.

Camera and QR codes

Puzzle sharing produces a QR code. Scanning one uses the Google Code Scanner built into Google Play services, so the app itself does not request camera permission and never receives, stores, or uploads any image. Only the puzzle's starting numbers are encoded in a QR code — nothing else.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is scheduled and shown entirely on your device. Nothing is sent over the network to deliver it.

Analytics

The app contains no analytics or tracking SDKs. We collect no usage statistics of any kind.

Children

Pocket Sudoku is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13.
